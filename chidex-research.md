# Chidex: Competitor & Reference Research

Research date: 27 Nov 2026. Covers who already sells sewing materials online, how they handle wholesale, and what that means for the Chidex site.

## 1. Who is doing something similar

### Nigerian sellers

| Business | What they do | What to take from them |
|---|---|---|
| **Jumia NG** (sewing / tailoring categories) | Marketplace with threads, shears, tapes, presser feet and machines, priced in naira | Category browsing, filters, pay-on-delivery. This is the experience Nigerian shoppers already know. |
| **MH (mh-chine.com) Nigeria** | Large Chinese haberdashery maker with offices in Lagos and Onitsha. Sells threads, needles, interfacing and ribbons | Huge catalogue organised by product type. Mostly a catalogue rather than a shop you can check out from. |
| **Fabriclore** (Indian, sells into Nigeria) | Bulk fabric sourcing for fashion businesses, low MOQ, swatches, says 3–6 day delivery to metro areas | Wholesale-first copy and "order a swatch first". It is the closest wholesale model. |
| **MyAsoebi.com.ng, FabricWay, Floxy Fabrics** (Lagos) | Small fabric shops with home delivery | Show how local shops sell online. Their sites are basic, which leaves room for Chidex to stand out. |
| **Complete Woman Fabrics, Tailornimi** (Lagos) | Tailoring materials, wholesale and retail | Same kind of business as Chidex but with almost no online ordering. That gap is the opportunity. |
| **TradeDepot / Omnibiz (OmniRetail)** | B2B restocking apps for small shops (groceries, not sewing) | The best Nigerian model for **bulk ordering**: order by app, WhatsApp or phone, free delivery, reorder, wallet. |

### International sewing-supply stores (good design references)

| Store | Why look at it |
|---|---|
| **WAWAK** (US) | About 24,000 notions for tailors and workrooms. Merged with Banasch's, a wholesale distributor. Free shipping over $99. Very close to what Chidex sells. |
| **Mood Fabrics** (US) | Polished store with a separate online wholesale programme. Wholesale minimums vary by product, and many start at 20 yards. |
| **Minerva** (UK) | Very large sewing-supply range, clean category navigation. |
| **Organic Cotton Plus** | Clear **tiered pricing**: discounts by yardage band, plus 8% off orders over $2,000 and 15% off over $10,000. |
| **Sew Kind of Wonderful** | Flat wholesale discount (50% off) once you pass a minimum order. This is the same idea as Chidex's ₦100k rule. |
| **Wazoodle** | Separate bulk tiers for notions and fabric. |
| **Faire** | Wholesale marketplace with a minimum per brand and payment terms. |

## 2. Patterns worth copying

1. **Two prices on every product**: retail in bold, wholesale shown underneath, for example "₦1,200 · Wholesale ₦950".
2. **Progress bar in the cart**: "You're ₦23,500 away from wholesale prices". Once the cart passes ₦100,000, every line switches to wholesale price and the bar shows the total saved.
3. **Sell by pack, carton and unit**: notions are sold in dozens, gross and cones, so offer a quantity box or pack options.
4. **Quick reorder / saved lists**: wholesale buyers buy the same items every time. This is what TradeDepot does.
5. **Payment the Nigerian way**: Paystack or Flutterwave for card, transfer and USSD, plus direct bank transfer for big orders, plus pay on delivery in Lagos.
6. **Delivery choice at checkout**: store pickup, "Chidex delivers" (fee by zone), or "I'll send my own driver/park".
7. **Order sent to WhatsApp**: Nigerian buyers trust it, and it gives staff an easy alert.
8. **Admin order board**: New → Confirmed → Packed → Dispatched/Picked up.

## 3. Open questions for Chidex

- Does ₦100,000 count the cart's **retail** total or its **wholesale** total? (Usually retail.)
- Are wholesale prices visible to everyone, or only to logged-in buyers?
- Delivery: which areas, who pays, and are interstate parks allowed?
- Payment: online only, bank transfer, or pay on delivery too?
- How many products are there, and who will upload and update them?
- Store address, hours, phone/WhatsApp, logo and photos for the About page.
